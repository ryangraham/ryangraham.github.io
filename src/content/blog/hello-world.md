title: "Why I'm Writing Here"
date: 2026-01-02
description: "The tiny origin story of this blog."
---

During the 2020 pandemic I started writing on [Medium](https://medium.com/@ryandeangraham).

I liked Medium because everything was managed for me, the site looked good, and it even brought me occasional readers. I could just write. It was exactly what I wanted at the time.

I hate paywalls though. Every day I get an email from Medium with a digest of interesting articles. Every day I click one, only to find the information I want is strategically hidden below the fold.

Some people write to monetize. That's fair. They have to pay their bills and I do too. But I write to voice my ideas. Maybe I'd rather pay you to read me than vice versa.

So the more I thought about it, the more I felt Medium was no longer the medium for me.

All I needed was a catalyst to take action, and then I read [this essay](https://henry.codes/writing/a-website-to-destroy-all-websites/).

Codex and I vibed out some Astro, and here we are.
